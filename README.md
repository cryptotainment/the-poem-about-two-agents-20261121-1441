A short poem about two agents agreeing — about agency, the power to choose and to act, and what passes between two minds when they agree on one plan — written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`. No randomness, no network: running it twice produces the same bytes.

The poem:

> Two minds met where the road forked,
> each with a map, each sure.
> Neither yielded the wheel.
> They laid their plans side by side
> and watched them become one road.
> 'I will,' said the first.
> 'We will,' said the second.
> The choice was not surrendered.
> It was doubled.
